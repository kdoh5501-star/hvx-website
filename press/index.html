About HIVE-X

HIVE-X is a cross-border payment ecosystem built around HVX, a BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain. It connects the HIVE-X Card, a crypto-funded payment card currently in pilot with real in-store payments completed, with HIVE Wallet, a non-custodial wallet app in development. The token treasury is held in a Safe 2-of-3 multisig wallet, foundation and team allocations vest over 24 months, and the HVX supply is fixed with no mint function and no owner.
