Security

Built to be checked, not just trusted

Every safeguard below can be verified on-chain by anyone.

Multisig treasury

The token treasury sits in a Safe wallet. Moving funds needs two of three independent signers.

Locked vesting

Foundation and team tokens are held by a vesting contract and release gradually over 24 months.

Fixed supply

The HVX contract has no mint function and no owner key. 100 billion is the final supply.

KYC for cardholders

Every cardholder completes identity verification before the card is issued.

Non-custodial access

You connect your own wallet. HIVE-X never asks for your seed phrase or private key.

Verified source code

Token and vesting contracts are published and verified on BscScan.

FAQ

Questions

Which wallets can I connect?

Any wallet that supports BNB Smart Chain, including MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet and Binance Web3 Wallet. On mobile, open this site inside your wallet's browser. Our own HIVE Wallet is in development.

Can I pay with HVX on the card today?

Not yet. The card is currently funded with USDT. HVX top-up is in development and will be announced on official channels.

Can more HVX ever be created?

No. The contract has no mint function and no owner. The supply is permanently 100,000,000,000 HVX.

Is HVX listed on an exchange?

Not at this time. Any listing will be announced only through official HIVE-X channels. Be careful of anyone offering HVX at a "pre-listing" price.

How do I avoid fake tokens and scams?

Only use the contract address on this page (0x252C…0028). HIVE-X will never ask for your seed phrase, private key or a transfer to "verify" your wallet.
